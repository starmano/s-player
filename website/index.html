S Player

Watch Stremio in a floating window that stays on top while you use your phone, like Netflix and YouTube.

Download for Android

Android 8.0 or newer · Free

Install

  1. Download the app on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file and tap Install. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps first.
  3. Choose it in Stremio: go to Settings → Player and pick S Player as the external player.

What it does

  • Picture-in-picture

    Go home while a video plays and it keeps going in a window you can move and resize.

  • Controls in the window

    Back 10 seconds, play or pause, and forward 10 seconds without opening the app.

  • Made for Stremio

    Plays Stremio's torrent and addon streams, including HLS and DASH.

Already installed? S Player updates itself: open the app and tap Install update. If Android says the app wasn't installed, uninstall the old S Player once and install it from here.
